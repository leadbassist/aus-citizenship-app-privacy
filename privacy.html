1. Summary

The App is an independent study aid for people preparing for the Australian citizenship test. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not sell or share personal information. Your quiz history, progress and settings stay on your own device.

The App is provided “as is”, as a general study aid only. It is not official, it may contain errors or be out of date, and it does not guarantee that you will pass any test. Please read sections 2 and 8 carefully.

4. Privacy: what we collect and where it lives

Stored only on your device. To work and to show your progress, the App saves on your device: your quiz results and answer history, a quiz in progress, Study Notes reading progress, your settings (theme, text size, explanations, haptics, animations, reminder choices) and whether you have unlocked the full version. We never receive this information. You can clear your history with Settings → Reset all stats, and uninstalling the App deletes everything.

What we do not collect. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, contact details, location, contacts, photos, microphone or advertising identifiers. We use no analytics or tracking tools and show no advertising.

Text to speech. The “Listen” feature uses your device’s built-in speech engine. The App does not record or send audio.

Children. The App collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It is intended for people preparing for the citizenship test.

Other websites. The App links to official government websites. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them, and we are not responsible for their content.

5. Feedback and crash reports (only if you send them)

Send feedback. If you choose Settings → Send feedback, the message you write, the type you select (problem, idea or question), your optional email address (only if you type one so we can reply), the App version and your phone model and operating-system version are sent to us through Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), a third-party service that delivers the message to our email. As with any internet request, Sentry’s servers may receive technical data such as your IP address. Do not include sensitive personal information in your message. Sentry handles data under its own privacy policy, and its servers may be outside Australia.

Crash reports. If the App crashes, it offers to send us a crash report. It contains the App version, your phone model and operating-system version, the screen you were on and technical error details. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. The report is delivered through our error-reporting provider, Sentry, which emails it to us. If that service is not available, the App opens an email to us in your own mail app instead, and you see the email before you send it.

We use feedback and crash reports only to fix problems, improve the App and reply to you, and we keep them only as long as needed for those purposes.

6. Study reminders, rating and sharing

Reminders. If you turn on study reminders, the App asks your permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself: nothing is sent to a server and no push token is created. You can turn them off in the App or in your phone’s settings at any time. We do not guarantee that a reminder will be delivered on time, or at all, because delivery depends on your device, its battery and notification settings.

Rating and sharing. Rate the app opens the App’s page in Google Play or the Apple App Store, or shows the store’s own rating prompt. Share the app opens your phone’s share sheet with a short message and a link. We do not see what you rate or whom you share with.
